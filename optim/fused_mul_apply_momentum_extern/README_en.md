## Function

- **Operator function**: a CANN graph-mode fused training operator. It fuses gradient scaling (Mul) and momentum-based parameter update (ApplyMomentum) into a single elementwise update, and updates the master weight `var`, the momentum buffer `accum`, and the low-precision weight copy `var_copy` **in place** (inplace semantics). It is generated by the `momentum_lossscale_fusion_pass` fusion PASS and matches the plus momentum semantics of TensorFlow `ApplyMomentum`. When `use_nesterov=True`, the operator uses Nesterov momentum.

- **Formula**:

  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  grad          &= x1 \times x2 \\
  accum_{t}     &= accum \times momentum + grad
  \end{aligned}
  $$

  - If use_nesterov = False (standard momentum):

    $$
    \Delta v = accum_{t} \times lr
    $$

  - If use_nesterov = True (Nesterov momentum):

    $$
    \Delta v = grad \times lr + accum_{t} \times momentum \times lr
    $$

  - Parameter update:

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    var_{t}       &= var - \Delta v \\
    var\_copy_{t} &= var\_copy - \text{Cast}(\Delta v) \\
    accum\_out    &= \text{Cast}(accum_{t})
    \end{aligned}
    $$

  Here `x2` is the scaling scalar of the reciprocal LossScale. The master weight `var` is always FLOAT (FP32) and is updated with full precision throughout; the low-precision copy `var_copy` is updated synchronously and independently (subtracting `Cast(Δv)` rather than `Cast(var_t)`, avoiding a second precision loss). The low-precision path (FP16) is computed in FP32 internally, and the output is cast back to FP16.

- **dtype path constraint**: `var` is always FLOAT; the dtypes of the five tensors `accum`/`lr`/`x1`/`momentum`/`x2` must be consistent, with values in {FLOAT, FLOAT16}; `var_copy` is always FLOAT16.

## Constraints

- The shapes of the four tensors `var`, `accum`, `x1`, and `var_copy` must be strictly consistent (elementwise computation, with no broadcast between tensors).
- `lr`, `momentum`, and `x2` are scalars with shape={1}, participate in the elementwise computation by broadcast, and do not support empty tensors.
- Only the Ascend 950PR&950DT products are supported (DAV_3510 / arch35, RegBase route).
